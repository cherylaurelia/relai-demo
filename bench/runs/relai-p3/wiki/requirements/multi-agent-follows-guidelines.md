# Multi-agent reports follow the task guidelines when enabled

With `follow_guidelines: true`, the multi-agent pipeline reviews each draft against the
`guidelines` list in the task and revises until they are met; human plan feedback, when
enabled, revises the plan.

Source: `multi_agents/README.md` (task.json documentation, `follow_guidelines` example at
`README.md:91`, revision caps at `README.md:69`).
