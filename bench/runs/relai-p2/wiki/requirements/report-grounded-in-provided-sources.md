# Reports cite only sources present in the gathered research

The research report must base claims on the gathered information and cite only sources that appear in it,
with an in-text hyperlink for each factual claim, figure or quote.

Source: `gpt_researcher/prompts.py:309-310` ("Do NOT cite sources that do not appear"), `:294`;
product promise of "factual" cited reports in `README.md` and `docs/docs/gpt-researcher/getting-started/introduction.md:13`.
